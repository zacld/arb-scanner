📈 Trading

Buy / sell / swap from any wallet, against real external liquidity only.

Available now, on the Wallets tab

Per-wallet Swap (mainnet only, via Jupiter) already works from each wallet's own tab in Wallets — quote first, explicit confirm to execute. That covers a single wallet trading its own asset, one at a time.

What this dedicated tab adds (Phase 2/3)

A unified source-wallet picker across all of a project's wallets (Funding, Main Holding, Wallet 1-4), slippage control, a transaction-status stepper, and the Funding wallet's initial market buy as just another instance of the same trade primitive — gated by the launch state machine's route/liquidity checks (§11-12 of the spec), not available until Phase 2 wires those up.
No fake trades, no internal wallet-to-wallet "trades" — those are Transfers, handled on the Wallets tab, and are never confused with market execution here.
